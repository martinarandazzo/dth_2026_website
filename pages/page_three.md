## Which open source project would be useful in your major, and why?

## oTranscribe.com
## About oTranscribe.com
oTranscribe is a free and open web-tool designed to help in the transcription of recorded interviews.







## How would oTranscribe help in my major: Human Security
I believe oTranscribe would be helpful not only in my major but in many other majors whose research involves more qualitative methods such as interviews.
In the case of my major, Human Security, which is under Anthropology we are encouraged to use qualitative research methods for our Master’s thesis. One of the most popular qualitative methods to carry on research is of course to conduct interviews.
For a Master’s thesis in Human Security interviews are very important and one student could interview from five to fifteen people on average, not including group interviews. Transcribing all of these interviews would consume a lot of time and energy which could also be allocated to the analysis and development of the student’s thesis given the limited time they have to write and submit their work.
